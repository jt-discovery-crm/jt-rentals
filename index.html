Questions

Do I need a deposit?

Yes. Each rental has a refundable security deposit, listed with the rates above. It is returned after the unit is returned on time and inspected.

Do you deliver?

Pickup is available for ACs, and delivery is available for a fee that depends on your area. Washer and dryer sets are delivered and hooked up.

What do I need to bring or sign?

A valid photo ID and a signed rental agreement. We test the unit with you before you take it or before we leave.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes, if the unit is still available. Contact us before your return time and extensions are charged at the current rate.

Which rental fits my room?

The window AC is rated for up to about 250 sq. ft. and needs a window it can sit in. The portable AC suits smaller rooms and windows where a window unit won't fit. Tell us your room size and window type and we'll recommend one.
